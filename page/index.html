What the pattern says
The next hire may not be in the truck. It may be the person who keeps the truck moving.

Owners often feel a field-capacity problem first. More jobs. More calls. More estimates. But the actual delay sits somewhere else.

A dispatcher does not replace the technician. An estimator does not replace the salesperson. They give both people more clean hours to do the work that makes money.

This is where global talent gets interesting. You can add real ownership without building another layer of US overhead.

What I would build next

Build the first pass. Hire the owner.

For a field-service company, I would not start with a giant AI project. I would start with the moment a new job enters the business.

01 · Capture

A call or estimate request comes in

The system records the request, extracts the customer details, and identifies the job type.

02 · Prepare

AI does the repetitive work

It creates the summary, drafts the follow-up, and routes the next step into the right queue.

03 · Own

A person closes the loop

The coordinator confirms the schedule, handles exceptions, and makes sure the customer gets an answer.

Recommended combination AI call intake + service coordinator or dispatcher
